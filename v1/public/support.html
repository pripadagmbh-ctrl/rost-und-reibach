  • How do I grab? Right half of the screen: push your thumb to the right to close the grapple, to the left to open it.

  • Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. If you delete the app, it is gone.
  • Does the game need the internet? No.
  • Are there ads or in-app purchases? No.
